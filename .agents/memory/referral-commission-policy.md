---
name: Referral commission eligibility
description: Which product purchases trigger referral commissions.
---

Referral commissions are earned only from a user's first paid purchase of a stable product. Activity-product purchases, free products, administrator-assigned products, and any later stable-product purchases do not trigger referral commissions. An earlier activity purchase does not prevent the user's later first stable purchase from qualifying.

**Why:** the user specified that referrals must be tied exclusively to the referred user's first stable-product purchase, not other purchases.

**How to apply:** determine eligibility within the locked purchase transaction using the product type saved at purchase time when available; process the existing referral levels only after a qualifying purchase commits.
