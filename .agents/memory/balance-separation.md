---
name: Balance separation
description: Accounting policy for non-withdrawable deposit funds and withdrawable user earnings.
---

The deposit balance is non-withdrawable. Move the full legacy balance into the deposit balance when introducing the split. Deposits, signup rewards, gift-code claims, check-in rewards, and other ordinary bonuses go to the deposit balance. Stable and activity product purchases are paid from it.

The withdrawal balance receives stable and activity earnings at maturity, referral commissions, and invitation-task rewards. Wheel prizes go to the non-withdrawable deposit balance. Withdrawal requests may use only the withdrawal balance; rejected withdrawals return funds to it. Administrators can adjust the two balances independently.

**Why:** The user specified these wallet boundaries so deposited funds and ordinary bonuses cannot be withdrawn, while earned rewards can.

**How to apply:** Route every credit, debit, refund, and purchase to its designated wallet. Never use the aggregate compatibility balance to authorize a purchase or withdrawal.
