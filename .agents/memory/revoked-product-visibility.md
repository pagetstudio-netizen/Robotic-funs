---
name: Revoked product visibility
description: User and admin visibility rules for positions revoked by an administrator.
---

Admin-revoked product positions must disappear from user-facing product lists, while remaining distinguishable to administrators. Do not confuse a revoked position with a normally completed one.

**Why:** the user wants revoked products hidden from the affected user, without asking to erase the purchase record.

**How to apply:** preserve the position record, mark revocation separately from completion, filter revoked positions from user lists, and retain their revocation status in admin views.
